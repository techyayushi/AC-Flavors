Saag

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mustard leaves (sarson)
  • 1 cup spinach (palak)
  • 1/2 cup bathua (optional)
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 2 tbsp maize flour (makki ka atta)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp ghee

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Wash and chop all green leaves.
  2. Boil greens with green chillies, garlic and ginger.
  3. Cool and grind into coarse paste.
  4. Heat ghee in a pan and sauté onions till golden.
  5. Add tomatoes and cook till soft.
  6. Add ground saag mixture.
  7. Add maize flour and cook on low flame.
  8. Stir continuously to avoid lumps.
  9. Cook till thick and aromatic.
  10. Serve hot with makki roti and butter.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Too bitter: Add more spinach or butter.
  • Lumpy texture: Stir well while adding maize flour.
  • Too thick: Add warm water gradually.
  • Raw taste: Cook longer on low flame.
