Dosa

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds (methi)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as required
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Wash and soak rice, urad dal and methi seeds for 6 hours.
  2. Grind to a smooth batter using water.
  3. Ferment batter overnight or 8–10 hours.
  4. Add salt and mix gently.
  5. Heat a non-stick or iron tawa.
  6. Pour batter and spread in circular motion.
  7. Drizzle oil around edges.
  8. Cook till golden and crisp.
  9. Fold and serve hot with chutney or sambar.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Dosa not crisp: Batter too thick — add water.
  • Dosa sticking: Tawa not hot or not seasoned.
  • No fermentation: Keep batter in warm place.
  • Sour taste: Batter over-fermented.
