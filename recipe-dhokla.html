Dhokla

Ingredients

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp Eno / fruit salt

For Tempering

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 2 green chillies

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Mix besan, curd, water, salt, sugar and ginger-chilli paste.
  2. Whisk till smooth and lump-free.
  3. Rest batter for 10 minutes.
  4. Add Eno just before steaming and mix gently.
  5. Pour batter into greased tray.
  6. Steam for 15–18 minutes on medium flame.
  7. Cool slightly and cut into pieces.

If Dhokla Doesn’t Turn Out Well 😅

  • Dhokla is hard: Batter was too thick — add little water next time.
  • Not fluffy: Eno not fresh or batter over-mixed.
  • Bitter taste: Too much Eno added.
  • Sticky texture: Under-steamed — steam for few more minutes.
