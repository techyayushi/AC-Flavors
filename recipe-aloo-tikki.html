Aloo Tikki

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes (boiled & mashed)
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves
  • 1 green chilli (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp cornflour / breadcrumbs
  • Oil for shallow frying

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Take mashed potatoes in a bowl.
  2. Add onion, coriander, green chilli, spices and salt.
  3. Add cornflour/breadcrumbs and mix well.
  4. Shape mixture into flat round tikkis.
  5. Heat oil in a pan on medium flame.
  6. Shallow fry tikkis till golden brown on both sides.
  7. Remove and serve hot with chutney.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Tikki breaking: Add more cornflour or breadcrumbs.
  • Too soft: Refrigerate mixture for 10–15 minutes.
  • Not crispy: Oil not hot enough while frying.
  • Less taste: Sprinkle chaat masala or lemon juice.
