Rajma

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rajma (kidney beans)
  • 3 cups water (for pressure cooking)
  • 2 onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Wash and soak rajma overnight (8–10 hours).
  2. Pressure cook rajma with water and salt till soft.
  3. Heat oil/ghee in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  4. Add onions and sauté till golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook till raw smell goes.
  6. Add tomato puree and spices.
  7. Cook till oil separates from masala.
  8. Add cooked rajma along with water.
  9. Simmer on low flame for 20 minutes.
  10. Sprinkle garam masala and serve hot.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Rajma hard: Beans undercooked — pressure cook longer.
  • Watery curry: Mash few beans and simmer more.
  • Too spicy: Add fresh cream or butter.
  • Raw taste: Masala not cooked properly — cook longer.
