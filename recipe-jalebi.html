Jalebi

Ingredients

For Jalebi Batter

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder or food color
  • Water as required

For Sugar Syrup

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 4–5 saffron strands
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Mix maida, cornflour, curd and baking soda.
  2. Add water and make thick, smooth batter.
  3. Cover and ferment batter for 8–10 hours.
  4. Prepare sugar syrup with one-string consistency.
  5. Heat oil or ghee in a flat pan.
  6. Pour batter into piping bag or bottle.
  7. Pipe spiral shapes into hot oil.
  8. Fry till crisp and golden.
  9. Immediately dip hot jalebis in warm syrup.
  10. Serve hot or warm.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Jalebi not crisp: Batter too thin or oil not hot.
  • Flat jalebi: Batter under-fermented.
  • Syrup not sticking: Syrup too thin or cold.
  • Too sour: Batter over-fermented.
