Matar Paneer

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer (cubed)
  • 1 cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Heat oil or butter in a pan.
  2. Add onions and sauté till golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook till aromatic.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook till oil separates.
  5. Add turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
  6. Add peas and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  7. Add paneer cubes and mix gently.
  8. Add water as required and simmer for 5 minutes.
  9. Sprinkle garam masala and switch off flame.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Paneer hard: Soak paneer in warm water for 10 minutes.
  • Gravy too sour: Add a pinch of sugar or cream.
  • Gravy thin: Cook longer till thickened.
  • Less flavor: Add kasuri methi or butter.
