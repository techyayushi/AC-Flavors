Litti Chokha

Ingredients

For Litti

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup sattu (roasted gram flour)
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 inch ginger (grated)
  • 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • Salt to taste

For Chokha

  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 1 large brinjal (eggplant)
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 garlic cloves (crushed)
  • 2 green chillies
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • Salt to taste

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Prepare dough using wheat flour, salt and water.
  2. Mix sattu with onion, garlic, ginger, chillies, ajwain, lemon juice and mustard oil.
  3. Stuff sattu mixture into dough balls and seal properly.
  4. Roast litti on charcoal, oven or tawa till cooked.
  5. Boil or roast potatoes, brinjal and tomatoes.
  6. Peel and mash vegetables for chokha.
  7. Add onion, garlic, chillies, mustard oil and salt.
  8. Mix well and serve with ghee-loaded litti.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Litti dry: Add more mustard oil or ghee.
  • Litti uncooked inside: Cook on low flame longer.
  • Bitter chokha: Remove burnt skin properly.
  • Dry chokha: Add mustard oil and mix well.
