Samosa

Ingredients

For Outer Cover

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 4 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as required

For Filling

  • 3 boiled potatoes (mashed)
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Mix maida, salt and oil to form crumbly texture.
  2. Add water slowly and knead stiff dough.
  3. Cover and rest dough for 30 minutes.
  4. Heat oil, add cumin seeds and ginger.
  5. Add peas, potatoes and spices.
  6. Cook filling for 5 minutes and cool.
  7. Divide dough, roll and shape samosas.
  8. Fill with potato mixture and seal edges.
  9. Deep fry on low flame till golden brown.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Samosa not crispy: Dough too soft — knead stiff.
  • Oil bubbles: Oil too hot — fry on low flame.
  • Samosa breaking: Seal edges properly with water.
  • Filling bland: Add lemon juice or chaat masala.
