Chole Bhature

Ingredients

For Chole

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas (soaked overnight)
  • 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp chole masala
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil

For Bhature

  • 2 cups maida
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as required
  • Oil for deep frying

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with salt till soft.
  2. Heat oil, add onions and sauté till golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook till raw smell goes.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook till oil separates.
  5. Add spices and cooked chickpeas with water.
  6. Simmer for 10–15 minutes for thick gravy.
  7. For bhature, mix all ingredients and knead soft dough.
  8. Rest dough for 2 hours.
  9. Roll and deep fry till puffed and golden.
  10. Serve hot bhature with chole.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Chole hard: Pressure cook longer with enough water.
  • Gravy thin: Mash few chickpeas and simmer.
  • Bhature not puffing: Dough too tight — knead softer.
  • Bhature oily: Oil not hot enough while frying.
