Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

For Jamun

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • Milk (as required for kneading)
  • A pinch of baking soda
  • Oil or ghee for deep frying

For Sugar Syrup

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 4–5 cardamom pods (crushed)
  • 1 tsp rose water

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Prepare sugar syrup and keep it warm.
  2. Mix milk powder, maida, baking soda and ghee.
  3. Add milk slowly and make soft dough.
  4. Divide dough and roll smooth crack-free balls.
  5. Heat oil/ghee on low flame.
  6. Fry jamuns till golden brown evenly.
  7. Transfer hot jamuns to warm syrup.
  8. Soak for at least 2 hours before serving.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Jamuns breaking: Dough too dry — add little milk.
  • Hard jamuns: Oil too hot or dough overworked.
  • Not absorbing syrup: Syrup too cold.
  • Uneven color: Fry on low flame patiently.
