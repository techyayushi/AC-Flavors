Palak Paneer

Ingredients

  • 250 g spinach (palak)
  • 200 g paneer (cubed)
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 1 green chilli (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Wash spinach and blanch in boiling water for 2 minutes.
  2. Immediately transfer to cold water to retain green color.
  3. Blend spinach with ginger, garlic and green chilli.
  4. Heat oil/butter and add cumin seeds.
  5. Add chopped onion and sauté till soft.
  6. Add spinach puree, turmeric and salt.
  7. Cook for 5 minutes on low flame.
  8. Add paneer cubes and simmer for 3–4 minutes.
  9. Sprinkle garam masala and switch off flame.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Dark green color: Do not overcook spinach.
  • Paneer hard: Soak paneer in warm water before adding.
  • Bitter taste: Spinach overcooked — add cream.
  • Too thick: Add warm water or milk.
