Veg Biryani

Ingredients

For Rice

  • 1½ cups basmati rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 green cardamoms
  • Salt to taste

For Vegetable Masala

  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato)
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp biryani masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee

How to Make (Step-by-Step)

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil rice with whole spices till 70% cooked. Drain and keep aside.
  3. Heat oil/ghee in a pan and fry onions till golden.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté well.
  5. Add tomatoes, spices and cook till oil separates.
  6. Add vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Add curd and mix gently.
  8. Layer cooked rice over vegetable masala.
  9. Cover and cook on low flame for 15 minutes (dum).
  10. Rest for 5 minutes, then mix gently and serve hot.

If Something Goes Wrong 😅

  • Rice mushy: Rice overcooked — cook only 70%.
  • Dry biryani: Sprinkle warm water before dum.
  • Less aroma: Add kewra water or saffron milk.
  • Masala too spicy: Add curd or fried onions.
